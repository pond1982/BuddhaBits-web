Support

Answers for iPhone, iPad and Android users.

How do I join on Android?

Use Android 8.0 or later with an up-to-date Android System WebView. Invited testers should use the same eligible Google account in the browser and Play Store. Join the closed test · Install the app

If you cannot open the link, check with the organizer that your email is on the tester list, then open the link with that account.

How do I change language?

Android follows the device language. On Android 13 or later, open Settings → Apps → Handbook for Mankind → Language. English on iPhone and iPad is part of version 2.1, currently awaiting review. Source texts and guided meditation audio remain in Thai, with English captions for guided practice in the bilingual update.

Can I read offline or with the sound off?

You can read 328 books offline. Three more open in the in-app reader with an internet connection. Linked recorded talks also need internet access. Lesson audio is optional.

Where is my journal stored?

Your entries are stored on your device. iPhone and iPad can also sync with your private iCloud when available. Android keeps entries locally. Use the journal’s export option to save a copy. Privacy

Is it free and do I need an account?

The app is free, has no ads or paid content, and requires no account for reading or journaling.
