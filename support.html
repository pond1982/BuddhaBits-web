Support

BuddhaBits is a small, quiet app for Buddhist learning, meditation, prayer, and reflection. If something is not working, thank you for taking the time to report it.

Contact

Email support@buddhabits.app.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead.

Please do not include private journal text unless you want it to be read for support purposes.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?

No. BuddhaBits does not require login.

Where are my journal entries stored?

Journal entries are stored on your device.

Can I use the app without audio?

Yes. Lesson narration is optional, and the app can be used fully by reading.

Is BuddhaBits a medical or therapy app?

No. BuddhaBits is an educational and contemplative practice app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Is the app free?

Yes. BuddhaBits is free and has no ads or paid content.
