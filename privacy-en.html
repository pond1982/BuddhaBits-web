Privacy

Updated 26 November 2026

Data on your device

The app requires no account, shows no ads and does not sell personal data. Your device stores lesson progress and level, streaks and completion dates, journal entries, meditation history, theme and voice settings, and reading activity.

Private iCloud sync on iPhone and iPad

From version 2.0, when iCloud is available, journal entries, highlights, bookmarks and reading positions can sync between devices through your private iCloud database. Completed lessons can also sync through your iCloud account. You can continue reading and writing locally when iCloud is unavailable. Android stores this data on your device.

Analytics and crash reports

The iPhone and iPad app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and improve reliability. These services may receive app interactions, diagnostic information, crash logs, performance data and installation identifiers.

Journal text is never sent to Firebase. Journal events may include the response length and whether you used a prompt or tag. The Android app does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Personal information

The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, voice recordings, payment information or journal text through the app.

External links

Links to source material, support sites and voluntary project support pages are governed by each destination’s privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If data practices change materially, the developer will update this policy and the store privacy information. For privacy questions, contact privacy@buddhabits.app
